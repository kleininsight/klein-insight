# Klein Insight editorial policy

Audience: Established, owner-led businesses. Positioning: evidence-grounded, candid business analysis focused on practical decisions.

## Rules
- Prefer a useful original lesson over a news recap. Explain what happened, what is established, what remains uncertain, why it may matter, and a practical diagnostic or exercise.
- Do not claim that a business trend applies to every small business.
- Do not fabricate client work, quotations, economic figures, case studies, or outcomes. No invented first-hand Klein Insight experience.
- News claims require attributable, directly relevant links to real sources. The owner must check each factual assertion and verify every link before approval.
- Exclude highly regulated personalized advice, rumors, celebrity content, political persuasion, and articles whose usefulness depends on speculation.
- Flag uncertainty and distinguish reported facts from interpretation. Avoid guarantees and misleading urgency.
- Do not copy paragraphs from a source. The result must be original educational analysis, not a rewrite of a copyrighted article.
- Keep news stories relevant to business operations, customer experience, marketing, research, hiring, technology, finance, or the economy.
- No publication without manual review and merge of the GitHub pull request.
- No direct publication to main, no automated merge, no auto-posting to social channels.
