90% of tech and data leaders plan to spend more on AI agents next year. Only 37% say they're seeing strong returns so far. That comes from a survey of 1,000 senior technology and data leaders by the data platform company Teradata: https://lnkd.in/eibSxRfz The main roadblock they name isn't the model. It's the data. 77% say 20% or less of their company's data is described well enough for agents to use. 78% say it's hard to connect data across business functions. Missing context (43%) ranked almost as high as data spread across systems (42%). An agent can only act on what it understands. If a field called "status" means three different things in three systems, the agent will pick one, and it may not tell anyone. So for product teams, getting the data ready isn't a side project. It's the roadmap. What that looks like in practice: Start with one workflow, not all the data. The report suggests picking the 20% to 50% that matters most and getting that agent-ready first. Write down what the key fields mean, who owns them, and when they go out of date. Make "the agent has the context it needs" a launch requirement, next to accuracy. Measure returns at the workflow level. The report found pilots often stop at personal productivity gains. Only 7% of the companies surveyed had both the data foundation and the governance to scale agents across the business. That's the group starting to see measurable results
