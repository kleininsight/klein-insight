Two families order the exact same dinner. For one, ordering in is the fun part of the night. For the other, it's the moment someone finally says, “I cannot make one more decision today.” Same transaction. Two very different motivations. That's where deeper consumer research can uncover opportunities that aren't obvious in the behavioral data. One family may respond to exciting new menu options, while the other may value easy reordering, family bundles, or anything that takes the thinking out of dinner. Understanding the need behind the behavior can change what we innovate, how we communicate and the experience we create.
