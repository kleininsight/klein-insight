I don't know if this is necessarily just a data problem. I've always said that AI is only as powerful as the understanding behind how it's being used. As you said, "An agent can only act on what it understands." But that raises another question: Does the organization itself fully understand the problem it's asking AI to solve? You can clean up the data, connect every system, and give AI access to everything, but if you don't understand the underlying business problem, you're still going to struggle to get meaningful results.
